Buyer FAQ

Questions international buyers ask.

Do you supply wholesale jewellery?

Yes. Uverra Gems is positioned for B2B wholesale enquiries, retailers, wholesalers and jewellery brands.

Can I request custom or private-label jewellery?

Yes. Share your design references, specifications and branding requirements so we can discuss suitable sourcing options.

Do you supply 925 sterling silver jewellery?

Yes. 925 sterling silver is a core catalogue category. Final specifications, weight, stones, availability and MOQ are confirmed before an order.

Can you ship internationally?

International B2B order coordination is available. Shipping, documentation and delivery details are confirmed according to the buyer and order requirements.

How do I get wholesale pricing?

Use the Buyer Inquiry form or WhatsApp and mention the product code, quantity and required specifications. We will discuss the appropriate quotation.
